Everywhere — the pool deck, the sala, the rooms, the kitchen, the toilets. Trash bags will be provided, so bin your rubbish as you go instead of leaving it for the end of the night.

06 Why we're doing this

Come to make a friend.

The point of this weekend is simple: connect and make new friends.

  • We want everyone to know each other by name. That's the entire reason for the nametags — not a formality, the whole idea.
  • This is not an orgy party. It isn't a place to hook up and leave. If that's what you came for, this isn't your event.
  • We're reimagining what #altercebu can be. A community built on friendship first, where people actually know each other.
  • Come in ready to talk to someone new. That's all we're asking. Everything else follows from that.

07 House rules

Five rules. That's it.

All of them are easy. This is a safe space for everyone, and these five rules are how we keep it that way.

  1. Wear your nametag the whole time. The goal is for everyone to know you by your name — that's the entire point of the weekend. Call other people by the name on their tag too.
  2. Everything here is voluntary. Join the games when you feel like it and sit out when you don't. Same with swimming, and same with sleep. Nobody is keeping score.
  3. Consent, every time. Respect everyone's personal space and always ask first. Sexual harassment will be dealt with accordingly. This is not an orgy. If intimacy comes up at all, keep it minimal and private.
  4. Ask before you take photos. Get the person's consent before any photo or video — and avoid posting them publicly afterwards. If you want to share something, ask the people in it first. Not everyone here is out, and not everyone wants to be findable.
  5. Share the space. Toilets and bathtubs are communal, so don't overstay. If more than one person is inside, locking the door is a major offense. Clean as you go, and look after the place — see Taking care of the place.

Something wrong? Tell an organizer immediately. Any incident, any concern, however small — don't sit on it. Someone from the team is on-site the whole 20 hours.

08 Strictly not allowed

No exceptions on these.

These four aren't negotiable, and they protect everyone here.

  • No drugs. Anyone bringing or using drugs will be reported to the authorities immediately. No warnings, no discussion.
  • No smoking or vaping inside the house. That includes the sala, the rooms, and every indoor area. Step outside instead.
  • No unregistered +1 guests. Walk-ins are not allowed. Everyone at the venue must be a confirmed, registered joiner.
  • No unsafe swimming. There is no lifeguard on duty, so avoid extreme or rough activity in the pool — particularly after drinking.
