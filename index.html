07 House rules

A wholesome barkada outing.

Let's be clear about what this is, so you know exactly what you're saying yes to.

01

This isn't an orgy party

Let's say it plainly: this isn't an orgy party. It's swimming, games, food, and chikahan with the community. If that's what you came for, this isn't the event for you.

Sexual harassment gets you removed from the venue and the community. No warnings, no exceptions. Everyone deserves to feel safe here.

Organizers are on-site for the full 20 hours. If anything feels off, tell us and we'll handle it. Dili ka mag-inusara.

04

Just be yourself

No performing, no explaining yourself, no pressure to be someone else. That's what Anonymous is for. Come as you are.

09 Questions

Ask us bisan unsa.

The common ones are here. If yours isn't, message us before you pay — we'd rather answer than have you guessing.

How many people are joining?

50 slots ra tanan — mao ni ang pinakadako nga event nato, ug limitado jud ang venue. Once mapuno, sirado na. Mas maayo kung sayo ka mo-reserve.

Unsa'y kalahian sa tulo ka packages?

Sunrise Chaser (₱1,200) — the whole 20 hours, Sat 3PM to Sun 11AM, dinner ug breakfast apil.
Cinderella (₱900) — same 3PM start with dinner, pero mo-uli ka by 12AM. Walay breakfast.
Nightcrawler (₱900) — 9PM pa ang entry, so walay dinner, pero apil na ang tagay, breakfast ug overnight.

Where exactly is the venue?

An exclusive resort in Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu. The exact location goes to confirmed participants lang since it's a private booking — kita ra jud tanan didto.

Is transportation included?

No, you handle your own trip. But we'll send complete directions once you're confirmed, so you won't get lost.

What should I bring?

Towel, toiletries, swimwear, extra clothes, and your personal stuff. If you're not getting a bed, bring your own sleeping gear pod.

What about food and drinks?

Dinner, breakfast, tagay and pulutan are all included. Walay dugang bayad once you're there.

Where do I sleep without a bed?

The sala sofas are open for everyone, or you can set up your own banig, sleeping bag, or tent. 20 ra ang beds (+₱500).

Do I have to stay the whole time?

Dili. Check-in is 3PM and checkout is 11AM, pero you can arrive later or leave earlier. Ikaw bahala.

What if I don't drink?

Okay ra kaayo, and more common than you'd think. Tagay is optional — ang chikahan man gyud ang main.

Can I get a refund?

Walay refund, pero it's transferable. Kung dili ka makaadto, pass your slot to someone else and let us know kung kinsa.

First time nako mo-join. Kulbaan ko — normal ba?

Normal kaayo. Most people who come are first-timers pod, and it usually goes away by dinner. The house rules are enforced and organizers are around all night — you won't be on your own.
