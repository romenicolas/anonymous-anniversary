03 Coming alone?

Good. Half the room will be a first-timer too.

You don't need to know anyone to belong here. That's kind of the whole point.

The program is built so you never have to be the person standing alone by the pool. The games at 4PM introduce you before you have to introduce yourself. Dinner sits you next to someone new. By the time the tagay starts, you're just part of the group.

06 Questions

Ask us anything.

The common ones are here. If yours isn't, message us before you pay — we'd rather answer than have you guess.

How many people will be there?

50 at most. Once we hit 50, registration closes — even if it's before the deadline.

Where exactly is the venue?

A private resort in Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu. We share the exact location with confirmed participants only, since it's a private booking.

Is transportation included?

No, getting there is on you. But we'll send complete directions once you're confirmed, so you won't be figuring it out alone.

What should I bring?

Towels, toiletries, swimwear, a change of clothes, and your personal essentials. If you're not getting a bed, bring your own sleeping gear too.

What about food and drinks?

Dinner, breakfast, tagay and pulutan are all included. Nothing extra to pay for once you're there.

Where do I sleep without a bed?

The sofas in the sala are open to everyone, or you can set up your own mat, sleeping bag, or tent. Beds are limited to 20 (+₱500).

Do I have to stay the whole time?

Not at all. Check-in is 3PM and checkout is 11AM, but you can come later or leave earlier. Do what works for you.

What if I don't drink?

Totally fine, and more common than you'd think. Tagay is optional — the chikahan is the real draw anyway.

Can I get a refund?

Payments aren't refundable, but they are transferable. If you can't make it, pass your slot to someone else and let us know who.

I'm nervous about going. Is that normal?

Very. Most first-timers feel it, and most say it's gone by dinner. Our house rules are enforced and organizers are around all night — you won't be on your own.
